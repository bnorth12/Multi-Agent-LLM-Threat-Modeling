- ID: HITL-013
- Text: Conditional gate state in the run record SHALL have one of three explicit values: (1) "Open" when condition is triggered and gate is awaiting analyst decision, (2) "Auto-Bypassed" when condition is not met and gate automatically closes, (3) "Rejected" or "Accepted" after analyst decision. The dashboard SHALL NOT report "Pending" for gates that are auto-bypassed.
