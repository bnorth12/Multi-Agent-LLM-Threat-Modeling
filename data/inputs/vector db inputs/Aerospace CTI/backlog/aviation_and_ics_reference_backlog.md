# Aviation and ICS Non-Conference Reference Backlog

## Purpose

Track high-value non-conference sources to improve real-world threat relevance beyond conference content.

## Priority Sources

- CISA ICS advisories and known exploited vulnerabilities where applicable.
- MITRE ATT&CK for ICS technique pages and procedure examples.
- FAA and EASA cybersecurity publications and safety-security guidance.
- NIST SP 800-82 and related CPS/OT guidance.
- Vendor PSIRT bulletins for avionics and supporting ground systems.

## Public-Only ISAC and Adjacent Intake (No Membership)

- Aviation ISAC public site: https://www.a-isac.com/
- Space ISAC public newsroom: https://spaceisac.org/newsroom/
- EUROCONTROL cybersecurity and EATM-CERT context: https://www.eurocontrol.int/cybersecurity
- EASA cybersecurity publication index: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/cybersecurity
- CERT-EU publication feed: https://www.cert.europa.eu/publications
- CISA KEV public machine-readable mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cisagov/kev-data/develop/README.md
