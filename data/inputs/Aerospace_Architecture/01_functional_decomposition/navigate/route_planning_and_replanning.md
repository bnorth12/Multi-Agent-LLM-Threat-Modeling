# Route Planning and Replanning

## Purpose

Plan mission route and dynamically replan under weather, airspace, performance, and mission constraints.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Generate Initial Route Plan
1. Evaluate Constraints and Hazards
1. Replan for Contingencies and Diversions
1. Coordinate Route Changes with Guidance and Communication Functions

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Corrupted planning constraints can produce unsafe or inefficient trajectories.
- Replanning logic abuse can force fuel or timing violations.
- Coordination failure with communication channels can desynchronize cockpit and ATC intent.
