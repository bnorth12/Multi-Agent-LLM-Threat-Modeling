# Internal Coordination and Mission Communications

## Purpose

Coordinate internal crew/system communication and, for missionized aircraft, mission-team information exchange.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Crew Coordination Messaging
1. System-to-System Coordination Signaling
1. Mission Team Data Dissemination [Missionized]
1. Information Sharing Policy Enforcement

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Cross-domain information leakage can violate mission and safety boundaries.
- Coordination-signaling tampering can induce mode mismatch between subsystems.
- Policy bypass can expose sensitive mission data flows.
