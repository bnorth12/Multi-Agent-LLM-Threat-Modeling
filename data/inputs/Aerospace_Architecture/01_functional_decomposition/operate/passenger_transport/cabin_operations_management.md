# Cabin Operations Management

## Purpose

Coordinate cabin systems and crew operational workflows supporting passenger safety and service delivery.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Cabin System Status Management
1. Cabin Crew Operational Coordination
1. Passenger Safety and Announcement Support
1. Disturbance and Incident Handling Support

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- False cabin status can drive inappropriate crew actions.
- Announcement-path manipulation can create safety confusion.
- Crew coordination integrity is required during abnormal events.
