# Commercial and Service Coordination

## Purpose

Support schedule, turnaround, and service coordination functions linked to passenger operations.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Ground-Service Coordination
1. Turnaround and Readiness Synchronization
1. Passenger-Service Data Exchange
1. Operational Event Reporting

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Service coordination disruption can create cascading operational delays.
- Data integrity issues can impact dispatch and readiness decisions.
- External interface trust boundaries must be explicit.
