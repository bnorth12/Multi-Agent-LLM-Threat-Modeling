# Mission Product and Dissemination Management

## Purpose

Generate mission outputs from fused sensor data and disseminate them to authorized consumers.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Generate Mission Products
1. Apply Product Classification and Handling Rules
1. Disseminate Products to Mission Stakeholders
1. Track Product Provenance and Revisions
1. Coordinate Mission Management and Product Prioritization
1. Publish Weapon-Employment Supporting Data Products

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Product tampering can alter operational decisions.
- Dissemination policy bypass can expose sensitive mission data.
- Provenance loss reduces trust in mission intelligence.
