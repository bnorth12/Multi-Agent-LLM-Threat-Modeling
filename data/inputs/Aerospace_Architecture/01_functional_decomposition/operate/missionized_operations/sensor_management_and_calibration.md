# Sensor Management and Calibration

## Purpose

Maintain sensor readiness, calibration quality, and configuration control across mission phases.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Manage Sensor Configuration Baselines
1. Execute Calibration Procedures
1. Validate Calibration Outcomes
1. Track Sensor Health and Drift

## L3 Sensor Families in Scope

- Radar, including multimode radar mission profiles.
- Hyperspectral sensors.
- Wide Area Motion Imagery (`WAMI`) sensors.
- `IRST` and `EO/IR` systems.
- Missile warning and radar warning systems.
- Electronic warfare and self-protection jamming subsystems.

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Calibration-data tampering can induce persistent mission-product bias.
- Configuration baseline corruption can invalidate mission confidence.
- Drift-monitor suppression can hide degraded sensing quality.
- Sensor-family-specific calibration corruption can cause cross-sensor fusion inconsistencies and false target confidence.
