# Pneumatic and Environmental Services

## Purpose

Support pressurization, air management, and environmental conditions required for safe operation.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Manage Pressurization
1. Manage Bleed/Airflow Paths
1. Regulate Environmental Conditions
1. Detect and Respond to Environmental Faults

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- False environmental state can drive unsafe control actions.
- Actuation-path corruption may degrade performance and crew/passenger safety margins.
- Monitoring resilience is required to detect latent faults.
