# Vehicle Systems Grouping

## Purpose

Provide a canonical grouping for aircraft utility functions commonly organized as Vehicle Systems.

## Included Utility Domains

1. Electrical power generation, conversion, and distribution.
1. Circuit protection and electronic breaker management (`ECCBUs`).
1. Pneumatic and environmental services.
1. Hydraulic services and actuation utility support.
1. Aircraft health and condition management.

## Boundary Notes

- Propulsion remains a distinct foundational flight service, but it is tightly coupled to Vehicle Systems utilities.
- Program-specific naming may vary (`Vehicle Systems`, `Utility Systems`, `Platform Services`) while functional intent remains equivalent.
