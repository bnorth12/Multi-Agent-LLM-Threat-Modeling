# Hydraulic Services

## Purpose

Provide hydraulic power and control to flight control and supporting actuation systems.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Generate Hydraulic Pressure
1. Distribute Hydraulic Power
1. Actuator Supply and Return Management
1. Leak/Fault Detection and Isolation

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- False pressure or flow telemetry can mask degradations.
- Distribution control compromise can selectively degrade maneuver authority.
- Fault isolation logic integrity is critical to survivability and graceful degradation.
