# Aircraft Health and Condition Management

## Purpose

Monitor aircraft system health, detect anomalies, and support safe maintenance and dispatch decisions.

## L2 Subfunctions

1. Acquire Health and Fault Data
1. Detect and Classify Fault Conditions
1. Generate Alerts and Maintenance Recommendations
1. Record Condition History and Trend Signals

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Health-state tampering can conceal latent hazardous failures.
- False fault injection can force unnecessary operational restrictions.
- Integrity of recorded history affects long-term airworthiness decisions.
