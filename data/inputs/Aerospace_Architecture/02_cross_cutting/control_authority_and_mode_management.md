# Control Authority and Mode Management

## Purpose

Define how control authority is allocated and transferred across pilot, automation, and mission systems.

## Authority Layers

1. Pilot primary authority
1. Flight-control augmentation authority
1. Autopilot guidance authority
1. Mission-system advisory authority

## Core Rules

- Authority source must be unambiguous at any time.
- Mode transitions require deterministic annunciation.
- Manual override pathways must remain available for safety-critical control.

## High-Risk Transition Cases

- Manual to autopilot capture.
- Autopilot disconnect under abnormal conditions.
- Missionized mode changes that influence flight or navigation priorities.

## Threat-Relevant Considerations

- Mode confusion is a major hazard and security-impact multiplier.
- Annunciation integrity is as important as command integrity.
