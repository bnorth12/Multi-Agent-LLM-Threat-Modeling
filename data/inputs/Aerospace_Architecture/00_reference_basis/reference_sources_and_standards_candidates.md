# Reference Sources and Standards Candidates

## Purpose

List reference families commonly used to validate aircraft functional decomposition and terminology.

## Reference Families

1. Safety and system-development guidance for aircraft functions and allocation.
1. Avionics and CNS/ATM operational concepts for navigation and communications context.
1. Maintenance, health-management, and operational procedure frameworks.
1. Mission-system references for ISR and missionized payload operations.

## Commonly Used Source Types

- Regulatory guidance and advisory material.
- Industry standards and recommended practices.
- OEM architecture references and interface-control documentation.
- Program CONOPS and mission-thread definitions.
- Operator maintenance and dispatch process baselines.

## Candidate Standards and Guidance (Non-Exhaustive)

- SAE ARP4754A and related development assurance guidance.
- RTCA DO-178 and DO-254 families for software/hardware assurance context.
- ARINC families (for avionics interoperability and partitioned systems context).
- CNS/ATM and operator procedure references for navigation/communication behavior.

## Usage Rule

Treat this file as a traceable source index. Add exact publication IDs and revisions when project-specific baselines are selected.

## Selected Cross-Reference Examples

These examples are illustrative anchors for future traceability work, not authoritative allocation statements.

- Flight-control authority transfer, annunciation, and degraded-mode handling map naturally to development-assurance and safety-assessment evidence families.
- Trajectory management, route integrity, and operational communication exchanges map naturally to avionics interoperability and CNS/ATM operational reference families.
- Domain isolation and partition-boundary enforcement map naturally to partitioned-systems and information-assurance reference families.
